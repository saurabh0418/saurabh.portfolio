About

I am a Software Engineer, entrepreneur, and community leader who believes in using technology and leadership to create meaningful impact. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Technocrats Institute of Technology & Science, Bhopal, where I was recognized as a University Topper and received the Excellent Achievers Award from the Vice Chancellor of RGPV and the Government of Madhya Pradesh. Professionally, I work as a Backend Software Engineer, building scalable and reliable backend systems and APIs for high-traffic platforms. My experience includes developing microservices, improving system performance, and working with modern backend technologies to support large-scale applications. Alongside my technology career, I am deeply involved in leadership and community service through Rotaract. Since starting my journey in 2020, I have served in several key roles including Club President, District Rotaract Secretary, and currently District Rotaract Secretary Admin for RI District 3040. My contributions have been recognized with awards such as Best Outstanding President, Outstanding District Rotaract Secretary, and Best Club Advisor. I am also the Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer of STaze Creations, a creative design firm delivering branding and digital design solutions. My journey has been honored with the India Book of Records and International Book of Records, reflecting my commitment to leadership, innovation, and social impact. I strive to combine technology, creativity, and service to build initiatives that positively impact both industry and society.
